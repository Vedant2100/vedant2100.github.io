What survives when a model learns something new?

A real, executed continual-learning benchmark. Five tasks arrive one after another: handwritten digits 0–1, 2–3, 4–5, 6–7, then 8–9. We compare updating model weights alone, replaying old experiences while updating, and retaining a small exemplar memory instead. All plotted numbers come from three executed seeds, not illustrative slider values.

Real provenance. Reproducible source.

The 1,797 images are from the public UCI Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits dataset loaded via sklearn.datasets.load_digits. We used an untouched 25% stratified test split per seed, 120 equal-count SGD minibatch updates per stage (32 samples/batch), and at most 160 stored real training samples. No mock, synthetic, or handmade benchmark records.
