## The question

Restaurant operations bring together related but distinct workflows: menu changes, reservations, orders, and payments. Those workflows need a shared state model while allowing different staff roles.

## Approach

In an IIT Bombay course project under Professor S. Sudarshan, I built a full-stack **React–Node.js** web app backed by REST APIs. It covered reservations, menu management, order and billing flows, and role-based dashboards with operational analytics.

## Evidence and results

The project is documented in my historical CV. Public source code, user-testing materials, and an active deployment are not available.

## Limitations
