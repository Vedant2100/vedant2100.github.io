## The question

Shared memory across agents raises organizational questions beyond retrieval: who can write a record, who is allowed to read it, how conflicts are resolved, and when it should expire.

## Approach

I organized the research space around memory provenance, access policy, update consistency, forgetting, and evaluation. The interactive [coding-agent memory lineage](/fleet-mem/lineage/) is one companion artifact; the [sharedmemgov repository](https://github.com/Vedant2100/sharedmemgov) tracks the broader institutional-memory map.

## Evidence and results

The map is a research synthesis and navigation aid, not a numerical benchmark. Its nodes and relationships should be read with the edge-definition and evidence notes provided alongside the interactive diagram.

## Limitations

A citation edge indicates a documented relationship only when checked; it does not automatically mean one method derives from another, and a map is never exhaustive.
