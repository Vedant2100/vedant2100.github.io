How to read this map

Solid line — explicit evidence. FrontierMemory's own October 2026 blog names or hyperlinks the task benchmarks, trajectory sources and contextual works. An explicit link does not mean code or methodology was inherited.

Dashed line — thematic lineage. Earlier Letta research is established by its research archive, but FrontierMemory's blog does not directly cite every earlier Letta post.

Dotted line — contrasting baseline. LoCoMo and LongMemEval are explicitly contrasted as recall benchmarks, not used as memory-dependent task sources.

FrontierMemory's published setup: 15 manually adapted tasks, with task/user/environment memory; Letta reports 219 relevant trajectories among 665 in its blog. The released v1.0 corpus used on the cluster has 674 trajectories. The sources do not reconcile the discrepancy. Gold memory is part of Letta's task-validation design, not independently confirmed by our 7B pilot.

Sources: Original research post · official code and task descriptions · Letta research archive. FrontierMemory v1.0 is currently cited as a blog/repository, not a verified peer-reviewed proceedings article.
